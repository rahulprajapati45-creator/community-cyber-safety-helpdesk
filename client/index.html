Important Safety Tips

A few golden rules to keep in mind every time you go online.

1

Never share your passwords or OTPs with anyone, even if they claim to be from your bank.

2

Think before you click — hover over links to check where they really lead.

3

Verify unfamiliar senders before opening attachments or downloading files.

4

Keep your apps, browsers, and operating system updated regularly.

5

Use official apps and websites only — avoid unverified third-party links.

6

If something feels wrong online, pause and ask for help before acting.
