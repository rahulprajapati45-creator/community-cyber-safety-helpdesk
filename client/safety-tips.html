Cyber Safety Tips

Simple, practical tips organized by category. Click a tab to explore.

1

Use at least 12 characters, mixing uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.

2

Never reuse the same password across multiple accounts.

3

Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on email, banking, and social accounts.

4

Use a trusted password manager instead of writing passwords down.

5

Change passwords immediately if a service reports a data breach.

6

Avoid using personal details like birthdays or names in passwords.

1

Be suspicious of urgent messages demanding immediate action or payment.

2

Check the sender's actual email address, not just the display name.

3

Hover over links before clicking to preview the real destination URL.

4

Never enter login details on a page reached through an unsolicited link.

5

Watch for spelling errors, generic greetings, and mismatched logos.

6

When in doubt, contact the organization directly using its official website.

1

Set your profiles to private and review who can see your posts.

2

Avoid sharing your home address, phone number, or daily routine publicly.

3

Be cautious accepting friend/follow requests from people you don't know.

4

Don't click on "you won a prize" or too-good-to-be-true contest links.

5

Report and block accounts that seem fake or behave suspiciously.

6

Think twice before posting photos that reveal your exact location in real time.

1

Only access banking sites by typing the URL directly or using the official app.

2

Never share OTPs, PINs, or CVV numbers with anyone, including "bank staff."

3

Check for "https" and a padlock icon before entering banking details.

4

Avoid online banking over public or unsecured Wi-Fi networks.

5

Turn on transaction alerts to monitor account activity in real time.

6

Log out completely after every banking session, especially on shared devices.

1

Review app permissions and revoke access you no longer need.

2

Read privacy settings before signing up for a new online service.

3

Avoid oversharing personal documents like ID cards or bills online.

4

Use privacy-friendly browsers or extensions to limit tracking.

5

Regularly review and delete unused online accounts.

6

Be mindful of what personal data quizzes and surveys ask for.

1

Only download software and apps from official stores or trusted sources.

2

Keep your browser and antivirus software updated at all times.

3

Avoid clicking pop-up ads claiming you've "won" something.

4

Double-check website spelling — scammers often mimic popular domains.

5

Use a reputable ad-blocker to reduce exposure to malicious ads.

6

Log out of sensitive accounts when using public or shared computers.
