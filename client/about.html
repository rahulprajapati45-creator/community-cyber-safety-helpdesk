About the Project

Learn why this platform exists and what it aims to achieve for our community.

Project Overview

The Community Helpdesk for Cyber Safety is an educational awareness platform created to help everyday internet users — students, parents, senior citizens, and small business owners — understand and avoid common online threats. It combines simple learning resources with a lightweight helpdesk so people have somewhere to turn when they encounter something suspicious online.

🎯 Our Mission

To make cyber safety knowledge accessible to everyone in the community, regardless of technical background, by presenting it in a clear and practical way.

🔭 Our Vision

A community where every internet user feels confident recognizing online threats and knows exactly where to turn for trustworthy guidance and support.

🌍 Why It Matters

As more daily activities move online — banking, shopping, communication — the risk of scams and data theft grows. Awareness is the first and strongest line of defense.

Project Objectives

What this platform sets out to achieve.

✔

Educate community members on common cyber threats in simple language.

✔

Provide practical, easy-to-apply safety tips across key digital activities.

✔

Offer a simple helpdesk channel for reporting cyber safety concerns.

✔

Encourage safer password, browsing, and social media habits.

✔

Reduce the community's vulnerability to phishing and online fraud.

✔

Promote a culture of "pause, verify, then act" before responding online.

Importance of Cyber Safety in Our Community

Cybercriminals increasingly target people rather than systems — using deception instead of technical hacking. A single unaware click can lead to financial loss or identity theft. By raising awareness at the community level, we help build a collective defense: informed individuals who look out for one another, share warnings about new scams, and know how to respond safely when something goes wrong.
