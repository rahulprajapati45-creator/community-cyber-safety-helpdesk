Common Cyber Threats

Understand how these threats work — purely for awareness and prevention, so you can recognize and avoid them.

Phishing Scams

Fraudulent emails or messages pretend to be from trusted organizations to trick you into revealing passwords, OTPs, or financial details.

Prevention

Verify the sender, avoid clicking unexpected links, and contact the organization directly through official channels if unsure.

Fake Websites

Scammers create websites that closely mimic legitimate ones to steal login credentials or payment information.

Prevention

Check the URL carefully, look for "https" and a padlock icon, and type addresses directly instead of following links.

Online Fraud

Fake online stores, lottery scams, and investment schemes lure victims into transferring money for goods or returns that never arrive.

Prevention

Research sellers and platforms before paying, be skeptical of deals that seem too good to be true, and avoid unverified payment requests.

Fake Social Media Profiles

Impersonation accounts are created to deceive friends and family, spread scams, or damage someone's reputation.

Prevention

Report suspicious duplicate accounts, avoid accepting requests from unknown profiles, and verify identity before sharing information.

Suspicious Messages

Texts, WhatsApp messages, or calls that create urgency ("your account will be blocked") pressure victims into hasty, unsafe actions.

Prevention

Pause before reacting to urgent messages, verify through official contact numbers, and never act on pressure alone.

Account Security Risks

Weak passwords, reused credentials, and unsecured devices make accounts easy targets for unauthorized access.

Prevention

Use strong unique passwords, enable two-factor authentication, and monitor account activity for anything unusual.

A Note on Awareness

This page is intended purely for educational awareness and prevention. We do not provide instructions for carrying out any of these activities — only guidance on how to recognize and protect yourself against them.
